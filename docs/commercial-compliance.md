# Commercial and public-repository compliance

1. Company brand, logos, real device images, product designs and internal test measurements require explicit authorization before public upload or release.
2. Record commercial-use licenses and attribution requirements for each model, font, soundtrack, voice and stock asset; absence of a clear license is not approval.
3. Do not misrepresent simulated spectra, GNSS performance, measurement screenshots or AI-generated results as real engineering test evidence.
4. Do not use a real employee's likeness or voice without consent.
5. Run technical, legal/IP and brand approval before company publication. Store approvals privately; public repo may contain non-sensitive signoff status only.
6. Check every commit for credentials, private addresses, customer names, internal design files and embedded metadata.
